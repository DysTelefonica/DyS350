# Release v0.0.2

Date: 2026-11-17
Tag: `v0.0.2`
State: the state of the environment of PROD (`DyS350_TF_E_PRO`) and of `main`.

## What was modified

### The JSON is the authority over the roles of an expediente

The child flow `E2E_AltaExpedientes_Modulo1` of PROD reconciles the roles of an expediente with the
JSON: what the JSON does not describe is removed.

| Flow | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `E2E_AltaExpedientes_Modulo1` (the child) | `3BD8762BC20F828C15877FAE4C1769CCC964BFA1FA840097BFF60EE97B164C52` | `E61C0AD9C0524FAA6D030EABEBF26CDCF868D8F8B372D3C4889CA9D634D609D7` |

The rules that PROD now applies, and that were measured in PROD with a controlled case of three
expedientes:

- A role row whose (person, kind) the JSON does not describe is **deleted**. The quality and the
  security rows are not protected any more: they used to survive for ever.
- The `Dirección` kind is **out of the scope of the flow**: it is never created, never updated and
  never deleted. It is set by hand.
- A row that already holds the person **and the name** of the JSON is **not written again**.
- The deletion **fails closed**: with no valid responsable in the JSON nothing is deleted.
- A defect that came with it: before this change the flow created a quality row with an **empty
  user** and added an empty login to the group when the JSON carried no quality person. Now the
  creation of the quality row has the same guard as the security one.

The promotion was a **port**, not a copy: the wiring of PROD (the eleven parameters of the
environment, the authentication of the 55 actions and the schema of the trigger) was kept and the
difference against the definition that was live in PROD is exactly the 18 paths of the phase.

The record of the promotion is in
[the record of the promotion](../changes/prod-role-authoritative-deletion-2026-11-17.md) and the
evidence of the work in the branch `DEV/CERT`, in `docs/tests/`.

## What was attempted and did not reach the release

- **The removal of the person from the group of the role** when the role row is deleted. The rule
  asks the deletion to take the person out of the group, and the code is written and ready
  (including the check that the person holds no row of that kind in another expediente, so that a
  person who is still the quality person of another expediente does not lose the access). It is
  **not published** because the REST API of this tenant does not expose the removal of a member of
  a group: `removeUser` answers 404 in its three forms and the deletion of the user of the
  collection answers 400 or 500. The addition to a group works and the flow already uses it, but
  publishing an action that fails would break the ingestion of every expediente whose role row is
  deleted. **The membership of the group is changed by hand until the endpoint exists.**

## What is left open

- The identifier of a role row still changes when the person of a role changes: the row is deleted
  and created again. The suppliers and the milestones already keep theirs since RF06.
- The business decisions **B5** (the duplicates of the leaf `900611367`) and **B6** (the identity of
  a milestone) are still open.
- The `Dirección` role keeps being set by hand, by decision of the team.
