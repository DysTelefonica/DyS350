## What was modified

### The person of a deleted role leaves the group of that role

The child flow `E2E_AltaExpedientes_Modulo1` of PROD takes the person out of the group when the
role row that the JSON no longer describes is deleted. It is the last rule of the phase F: the JSON
is the authority over the roles of an expediente, and an access that belongs to a role that no
longer exists has to go with it.

| Flow | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `E2E_AltaExpedientes_Modulo1` (the child) | `E61C0AD9C0524FAA6D030EABEBF26CDCF868D8F8B372D3C4889CA9D634D609D7` | `97D20BA24E01CEEB3DFF506C532FF3BE0F26C1EBE41E100AC73C0E0A51B5A1E9` |

The rules that PROD now applies on top of the ones of `v0.0.2`:

- The person of a role row that the JSON no longer describes **leaves the group of that kind**.
- **Only when she holds no row of that kind in another expediente**: the check that stops the flow
  from taking the access away from a person who is still the quality person somewhere else.
- The removal is made **by the identifier of the member of the group**, which is the form of the
  endpoint that answers. The addition of a member, which the flow already did, is unchanged.
- **The ingestion does not break**: a controlled case in PROD ended with `Total 3, Nuevos 0,
  Actualizados 1, Fallidos 0, Antiguos 0` and the group of the quality went from eight members to
  seven.

The promotion was a **port**, not a copy: the wiring of PROD (the eleven parameters of the
environment, the authentication of the actions and the schema of the trigger) was kept and the
difference against the definition that was live in PROD is exactly the five paths of the phase.

The record of the promotion is in
[the record of the promotion](../changes/prod-role-group-removal-2026-11-18.md) and the evidence of
the work in the branch `DEV/CERT`, in `docs/tests/`.

## What was attempted and how it ended

The removal of the member of the group was attempted **five times** in DEV/CERT before it worked.
Every attempt was measured with a controlled case and every failure said where the problem was:

| Attempt | What it changed | What was measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RF17 | one `If` with an `or` of the two kinds | the deletion worked, the group did not change: the action failed without running its branches |
| RF18 | one `If` per kind, without `or` | the condition of the project managers was evaluated without any error and only the branch of the quality failed: the endpoint `removeUser` does not answer |
| RF19 | the removal by the identifier of the member | the request of the member failed in 18 milliseconds, without reaching SharePoint: the connector decodes the URI and the `#` of the claim cuts the query |
| RF20 | the filter by the email of the member | the request failed again in 18 milliseconds: the expression of the login was in the URI without braces and it was sent as a literal |
| **RF21** | **the same, with the expression wrapped in braces** | **the person left the group and the ingestion did not break** |

The conclusion that was wrong at first ("the REST API of this tenant does not expose the removal of
a member of a group") is now corrected in the record: the removal that answers names the
**identifier of the member** and not the person.

## What is left open

- A member of a group whose row was deleted by other means (a row deleted before this rule was
  published) stays in the group: the flow only takes out the members of the rows it deletes.
- The identifier of a role row still changes when the person of a role changes: the row is deleted
  and created again. The suppliers and the milestones already keep theirs since RF06.
- The business decisions **B5** (the duplicates of the leaf `900611367`) and **B6** (the identity of
  a milestone) are still open.
- The `Dirección` role keeps being set by hand, by decision of the team.
